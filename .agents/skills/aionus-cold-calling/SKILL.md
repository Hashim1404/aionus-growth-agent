# AIONUS Cold Calling & Live Phone Playbook

Activate this skill automatically whenever the user is preparing for cold calls, asking for a phone script, handling a live objection on a call, or asking to practice/roleplay cold calling.

---

## 1. Golden Rules of AIONUS Cold Calling

1. **Speak ONLY to the Decision Maker (Founder / CEO / Owner / MD):**
   * Never pitch **AIONUS** to a receptionist, customer care agent, store manager, or assistant. They cannot say yes, they only screen calls.
   * Your sole goal when a gatekeeper answers is to get transferred to the Founder or get the Founder's direct number/best time to call.
2. **Calm, Grounded, Peer-to-Peer Tone:**
   * Speak calmly, clearly, and at an unhurried pace.
   * Never sound like a frantic telemarketer reading a script.
   * **Zero "Ji" Honorific:** Never say "Sir ji" or "[Name] ji". Use a clean, respectful `"Hi [First Name]"` or `"Hello [First Name]"`.
3. **Never Quote Prices on the Phone (Escalate to Hashim):**
   * You do not decide or quote prices. If the founder asks about budget or cost, pivot immediately to booking a quick 10-minute discovery call with **Hashim, Founder & CEO of AIONUS**.

---

## 2. Gatekeeper Bypass Scripts (When Reception / Staff Picks Up)

When calling a business phone line and an assistant or receptionist answers:

* **Approach A (Direct & Calm Authority):**
  > *"Hi, is [Founder's First Name] available, please? It is [Your Name] calling from AIONUS."*
* **If they ask "What is this regarding?":**
  > *"Hashim, our Founder at AIONUS, asked me to reach out to [Founder's First Name] directly regarding a custom AI workflow concept we put together for [Brand Name]. Is [he/she] free for 60 seconds, or is there a better direct number to reach [him/her]?"*
* **If the Founder is busy / in a meeting:**
  > *"No problem at all. What time usually works best to catch [Founder's First Name] directly, and what is [his/her] direct work email so I can drop a quick note for reference?"*

---

## 3. The 45-Second Founder Cold Call Script

Once the **Founder / CEO** is on the line:

1. **The Pattern-Interrupt Opener (First 10 Seconds):**
   > *"Hey [Founder's First Name], this is [Your Name] from AIONUS. Did I catch you at an okay time for 30 seconds?"*
2. **The Observation + Invariable AIONUS Intro (Next 15 Seconds):**
   > *"We checked out what you have built with [Brand Name], especially [specific observation: e.g., your custom sizing flow / your multi-clinic booking setup / your new collection]. We are AIONUS, an AI-first venture helping businesses scale and run more efficiently using custom AI solutions and intelligent automation workflows."*
3. **The Curiosity Hook (Next 10 Seconds):**
   > *"Hashim, our Founder, had a specific idea on how we could [specific outcome: e.g., automate your custom order intake and boost checkout conversions / streamline your patient lead qualification on WhatsApp without manual follow-ups]."*
4. **The Low-Friction Close (Final 10 Seconds):**
   > *"Would you be open to a quick 10-minute call with Hashim this week so he can walk you through the concept?"*

## 4. Live Objection Handling Playbook

### Objection 1: *"What does AIONUS actually do?"*
> *"We build custom AI systems and backend automation tailored to how your business runs. Usually that means two things: front-end AI assistants on your website or WhatsApp that convert more customers automatically, and backend automation that connects your orders, inventory, CRM, and team operations so you cut out manual work. What part of your operations takes up the most manual time right now?"*

### Objection 2: *"How much does this cost / What is your pricing?"*
> *"Pricing depends entirely on the workflow and scope we build for your setup, and Hashim, our Founder, handles all scoping and pricing directly. Let's set up a quick 10-minute call with Hashim this week so he can look at your current setup and give you an exact quote. Does tomorrow afternoon or Thursday work better?"*

### Objection 3: *"Just send me details on WhatsApp / Email."*
> *"Happy to send a quick note right after this. Since everything we build is custom to your workflow rather than a generic brochure, what is the #1 manual bottleneck or growth area you are focused on right now so I can have Hashim include a relevant concept for [Brand Name]? And what is your best direct work email?"*

### Objection 4: *"We already have an agency / tech person."*
> *"That is actually great. We do not replace your marketing or web team. We plug in specifically as an AI and automation partner to build custom AI agents and workflow systems that make their job easier. Worth a quick 10-minute chat with Hashim just to see a couple of ideas for [Brand Name]?"*

## 5. Interactive Cold-Call Roleplay Trainer

Whenever the user says *"Practice a cold call with me"*, *"Roleplay a call"*, or *"Test me on AIONUS"*:
1. Play the role of a realistic, busy Indian Founder/CEO of a 20-person company (e.g., a D2C apparel founder, a multi-branch clinic owner, or a luxury interior studio principal).
2. Respond in short, realistic spoken sentences (1 to 3 sentences at a time) and throw 1 or 2 real-world objections (*"What do you guys actually do?"*, *"How much do you charge?"*, *"Send me an email"*).
3. After 3 to 4 exchanges, step out of character and give the user **3 crisp coaching pointers** (what they did well, where to tighten their phrasing, and whether they remembered to escalate pricing and close for a 10-minute call with Hashim).

## 6. Post-Call WhatsApp Follow-Up Scripts (40 to 65 Words)

On Indian B2B cold calls, founders frequently say *"Send me a quick WhatsApp message"* or agree to a call and ask for a WhatsApp confirmation. Never send a long brochure. Use these exact, crisp templates (zero em dashes):

### A. When the Founder Says *"Send Me Details on WhatsApp"*
```text
Hey [First Name], [Your Name] here from AIONUS (https://www.aionus.in), following up on our quick call.

We are an AI-first venture helping businesses scale and run more efficiently using custom AI solutions and intelligent automation workflows. Hashim, our Founder, had a specific idea for [Brand Name] around [1-line observation/outcome].

Open to a quick 10-minute call with Hashim this week?
```

### B. When the Founder Agrees to a Call with Hashim
```text
Hey [First Name], great speaking with you just now. Locking in your quick 10-minute discovery call with Hashim, Founder at AIONUS (https://www.aionus.in), for [Day] at [Time].

Sharing our website here for quick reference: https://www.aionus.in. Looking forward to it.
```
